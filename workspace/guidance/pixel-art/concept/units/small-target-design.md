This design will be miniaturized into a tiny sprite (a single square tile, about 16x16 pixels), so it must make
the most of every pixel. Design for the tiny version, and be creative about the brief to get there:
- Fill the square. The whole silhouette should be compact and close to square, occupying most of the frame in
  both width and height. No long horizontal or vertical extent that leaves the rest of the square empty.
- No thin protrusions. Long, skinny parts (barrels, beaks, antennae, stick legs, tails) become 1-pixel dashes or
  vanish. Shorten and thicken them: snub-nosed, stubby, chunky versions that still say what they are.
- Compress or fold limbs into the body mass: legs folded up and tucked (feet just under the body), arms hugged
  close, so they add bulk instead of empty gaps.
- Exaggerate the one or two features that identify the character (make them big and bold), and simplify or drop
  everything else.
- Few large color masses: one main body colour, one accent, a bright eye. No fine patterns or small details.
- Strong outline and value contrast between the big masses, so it reads from its silhouette alone.
